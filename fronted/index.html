Each faculty demo account can manage only its assigned year and its two semesters.

Bring your own cohort

Import monthly student data

Choose a month in the top bar, then upload its CSV. Each month keeps its own student percentages.

Required columns

  • attendance_pct — attendance percentage, 0–100
  • midterm_score — score, 0–100
  • assignment_delay_days — average days late, 0 or more
  • lab_viva_score — score, 0–100

Helpful optional columns

  • student_name (shown in the dashboard), student_id, department
  • academic_year (1–4; defaults to your assigned year), semester (1–8; defaults to the selected semester)
  • month_in_semester (1–6; defaults to the selected month, and must match it)
  • tutorial_sessions, risk_status (metadata only)
  • attendance_week_1 … attendance_week_8
  • test_week_1 … test_week_4
A note on student privacy. Demo names are fictional. If a CSV includes student_name, that name is displayed with its session-only roll number and is personal data—not anonymized data. Monthly snapshots persist in this browser only. Use real names only with institutional approval and appropriate consent; do not upload identifiable student records to a public demo. This prototype is not a validated or production-ready academic decision system.
